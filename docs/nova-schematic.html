The NuMI target, the NOvA Near Detector, the NOvA Far Detector at Ash River and the MINOS Far Detector at Soudan, with the chords and angles the timing calculation uses. The ND and FD are the upstream-face centres surveyed by the Fermilab alignment group (NuMI beamline coordinates from MCZERO); the beam axis is the line from the target through the MINOS Far Detector. Lengths and angles are exaggerated so the geometry can be read; every number printed is the computed value.
